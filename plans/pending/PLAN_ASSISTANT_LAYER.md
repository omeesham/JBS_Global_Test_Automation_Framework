### A8 — The Rival Collaboration Engine (Rutvik 2026-07-11: rivals cancel each other's LLM weaknesses on EVERYTHING; seats are a dial, not a pair)

**Intent**: wherever quality matters, N adversarial seats (different families — their blind spots don't overlap) work the SAME thing, attack each other until they AGREE, then hand Claude ONE pre-reconciled result + the map of what they couldn't settle. Claude's interrogation surface collapses to that map. This is the layer's general quality engine, not a task-type feature.

**A8.1 — Collaboration-intensity dial (the efficiency guard — heavy machinery NEVER fires on a 1-line change).** Intensity is a cheap pure function of fields already on the brief (work-type, risk, protected/publishing touch, Rutvik-importance tag, seat self-flagged uncertainty) — NO extra dispatch to decide:
- **I0 solo** — DEFAULT: mechanical edits, deterministic verifies, small drafts, searches. One seat.
- **I1 pair-debate**: plans, designs, high-risk/protected reviews, RCA, multi-phase execution — the A8.3 protocol.
- **I2 pair + third-seat tiebreaker**: an I1 debate that deadlocks at R3 pulls the next-best THIRD family to read both positions + evidence and argue a resolution — Claude still final-gates. (Claude adjudicates directly when no third family is proven-available.)
- **I3 panel-of-3 independent + converge**: reserved for the heaviest calls (rulebook changes, protected-artifact promotion reviews) — three families work blind then reconcile pairwise. Rare by design.
- Intensity is stamped on the brief (`MODE: I0|I1|I2|I3`) + recorded, so the scorecard can show what each intensity level actually catches vs costs — the dial's thresholds themselves become tunable-by-evidence (proposals, Rutvik-gated).
- Seats may self-escalate ONE level on self-flagged uncertainty (I0→I1, I1→I2), never skip levels, never silently de-escalate.

**A8.1b — Application surface (deliberately unbounded — anywhere seats can cancel weaknesses, stitched into existing hops):**
- **Understanding duels (Hop-0)**: both seats independently restate the brief + plan; the DIFF of their restatements exposes ambiguity in the brief itself BEFORE work starts — catches vision-communication gaps at the cheapest possible point.
- **Deliverables + runtime decisions**: as specced (A8.3, A8.6).
- **Interrogation design**: both seats generate trap questions for a third worker's output; Claude fires the UNION — the asswhooping arsenal itself gets rival-built.
- **Adversarial test design**: one seat designs tests to BREAK the other's implementation (blind-parity spirit) — the builder never grades its own homework.
- **Premortem risk forecasting**: on heavy goals both seats independently predict failure points; overlap = high-confidence risks to guard, disjoint = surfaced blind spots.
- **Compaction integrity**: both seats summarize the same heavy report; the diff exposes omissions — protecting the exact channel Claude relies on to read the world.
- **Self-audit (MERGED into A9.7 per slop-trim §E)**: the engine's own protocol/weakness-map staleness is NOT a separate periodic exam — it rides the A9.7 gap-hunt cadence as one agenda item (collapsing 3 periodic self-exams to the A9.7 sweep; the D12c staleness nags cover the rest). Improvement proposals stay Rutvik-gated.

**A8.1c — Weakness map (makes "cancel each other's weaknesses" OPERATIONAL, not hoped-for).** `~/.claude/delegation/weakness-map.md` — per model FAMILY, observed failure patterns mined from real bounce/debate/lesson history (e.g. "family X invents file paths under length pressure", "family Y rubber-stamps its own regex"). Cross-exam rounds PRIME each seat with its opponent's documented weaknesses — attention aimed at known blind spots, not generic skepticism. Fed by debate outcomes + reviewer lessons; staleness-nagged like every other living file; family-keyed so new models inherit their family's map until they earn their own rows. Dynamic, never static-expiring.

**A8.2 — The pair (dynamic, cross-family).** `scorecard.mjs select` returns the **top-2 proven models of DIFFERENT families** for the work-type (today → gpt-5.5 + claude-sonnet-4.6, or opus-tier for the hardest). Different families is a HARD constraint, not a preference — same-family rivals share failure modes and would collude blind. One is LEAD (higher scorecard rank; logged), the other CHALLENGER — the role only decides who fires sub-work, not who wins a debate point. Never hardcoded; a new strong family auto-enters the pool.

**A8.3 — Debate protocol (bounded rounds; one-shot-dispatch-honest — rounds via the existing `--interrogate` cross-feed, NOT fake live chat).**
- **R1 — independent (parallel, 2 slots):** A and B each produce their full answer BLIND to each other.
- **R2 — cross-examination (parallel):** each is fed the other's R1 answer and must (a) attack every weakness, (b) revise its own answer, (c) emit a machine-readable block: `CONVERGENCE: AGREED|NO` + `CONCEDED: [what I changed after seeing them]` + `OPEN_DELTAS: [{point, my_position, their_position, evidence_needed}]`. Every resolved point must CITE the artifact/oracle that settled it — "we agree" without evidence is rejected.
- **Convergence check (Claude, cheap):** both AGREED + both OPEN_DELTAS empty + final answers match on material points → CONVERGED → the pair emits ONE reconciled answer + the debate trail.
- **R3 (only if not converged):** one more exchange on the OPEN deltas ONLY. Still open after R3 → STOP; hand Claude the two positions + the delta map; **Claude adjudicates** (the manager's irreducible job — debate is never infinite).
- **Round cap = 3** (R1 + ≤2 exchanges), early-exit the instant they converge (usually R2). Compute stays bounded.

**A8.4 — Delegation re-fight.** When the converged result needs sub-work, the LEAD dispatches it. If a sub-result is itself heavy → A and B RE-DEBATE the integration (same protocol, converges fast on an agreed frame); trivial sub-results the lead just integrates. Dual-rival does NOT recurse into every sub-task (slop-guard).

**A8.6 — Decision debates (Rutvik 2026-07-11: "things decided real time by an agent can be fought over" — model choice is the sample; scope = every runtime decision the layer makes).** The debate applies to DECISIONS, not just deliverables: model/tier selection, risk + OFF-REPO classification, escalation choices, bounce-vs-accept calls, timeout/scope judgments. Stitched into what exists — three tiers, cheapest first:
- **Tier 1 — rulebook decides (free)**: if `routing-policy.json` + scorecard + doctrine yield ONE clean answer (e.g. cheapest proven model for a routine build), no debate — the rulebook IS the two assistants' past agreements crystallized.
- **Tier 2 — rivals debate (cheap, bounded)**: the rulebook is silent, ambiguous, or the decision is high-blast-radius (protected artifact, new model, no proven candidate, conflicting rules, novel situation) → both rivals argue it to convergence (same A8.3 protocol; decision debates are naturally short — usually 1 exchange). The agreed decision ships with its WHY (one-para rationale + evidence cited).
- **Tier 3 — Claude/Rutvik**: rivals can't converge, or the decision touches the never-delegate list → Claude adjudicates; rulebook CHANGES stay propose-only to Rutvik, always.
- **Feedback stitch**: every Tier-2 agreed decision is logged (`decision-debates.jsonl`: {ts, decision_type, positions, agreed, evidence}); recurring identical agreements auto-surface as rulebook proposals ("rivals agreed 4× that model X handles work-type Y → propose proven") — the debates FEED the learning layer, Rutvik still approves every rulebook change.

**A8.5 — Anti-collusion / anti-false-convergence (the load-bearing guard).**
- **Debate trail is mandatory**: the report shows each rival's R1 position + exactly what it CONCEDED per round. If BOTH sides conceded nothing across all rounds → suspicious (truly-identical is rare) → Claude auto-interrogates that specific case.
- **Evidence-cited convergence** (above) blocks lazy "sounds-fine" agreement — **hardened per "The Consistency Illusion" (arXiv 2606.08457, Jun 2026): surface agreement after debate ≠ reasoning alignment, so the evidence-cited-convergence guard is "correct but insufficient" on its own. Each converged point MUST cite the specific oracle/artifact that settled it (A8.3 already half-requires this — here it is MANDATORY, not "should"); a convergence with any point lacking a per-point oracle citation is rejected and auto-interrogated.**
