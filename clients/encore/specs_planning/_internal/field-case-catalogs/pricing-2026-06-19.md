## 3. Net-new (a) — CAD/MXN per-currency primary dropdowns (office 1605) — IMPLEMENTED 2026-06-19

The only MAIN-scope net-new gap. Office 1604 is USD-only (5 dropdowns); office 1605 is multi-currency (15 dropdowns = 5 USD + 5 CAD + 5 MXN; testid keys `location-settings-select-primary-{labor,equipment,internal-equipment,production-labor,production-equipment}-pricing-{cad,mxn}`). Per the user multi-currency mandate (2026-06-15) CAD/MXN are **in-scope for this MAIN subplan, not deferred**.

**Live walk of office 1605 (2026-06-19) — what's actually selectable** (LR-015 / LR-061 — read, not assumed):
- All 15 primary dropdowns render and are enabled (Corporate Pricing checked). Corp checked, Include Service Fee **unchecked** (1605 default differs from 1604's checked).
- USD: all 5 populated. **CAD: all 5 empty** ("No pricing strategy found."). **MXN: Labor (100+ options) and Equipment (88) populated; Internal Equipment / Production Labor / Production Equipment empty.**
- So only **2 of the 10** CAD/MXN dropdowns (MXN Labor, MXN Equipment) carry strategies to select + persist. The catalog's earlier "select-commit/persist for all 10" assumption was wrong; this is the corrected, evidence-based scope.
- Both selectable MXN dropdowns were driven end-to-end live: select → save (HTTP 200) → reload → value persisted → restored to unset (office left clean). Verified for both Labor (`MEX DYN LB1 MXN 2025`) and Equipment (`MEX BO CDMX MXN 2025`).

**Implemented (Phase 3, office 1605):**
- Selectors: added the 10 `*-cad` / `*-mxn` keys to `src/selectors/locations/pricing.ts`.
- Data: `MULTI_CURRENCY_OFFICE_NO`, `PRIMARY_PRICING_DROPDOWNS_CAD/_MXN`, `MXN_PRIMARY_PERSISTENCE_CASES` in `src/data/locations/location-pricing.ts`.
- Tests (own describe, office 1605, no `@fcc` tag, sequential IDs): **TC-036** asserts all 15 dropdowns render + enabled; **TC-037 / TC-038** select-and-persist MXN Labor / Equipment via `saveAndVerifyCase()` with cleanup restoring unset.
- The 8 empty CAD/MXN dropdowns have render coverage (TC-036); select+persist is not possible (no options) — honest ceiling, recorded here and in the TC-036 Notes.
