## 8. CORRECTION — multi-currency + dropdown drivability (later same session, 2026-06-18)

Deeper collaborative re-walk (with user) **corrects** §1/§2 above:

- **Primary pricing dropdowns ARE drivable** — they're cmdk command palettes (`role=dialog` popover + search `<input placeholder="Search pricing strategies...">` + option `<button>`s). Drive via: open → `fill` the search input → `click [role=dialog] button:has-text('<option>')`. Value commits + Save enables + **round-trips on save** (proven: 1604 Labor `2026-Zone 3 E` persisted). My earlier "selection won't commit" was operator error (stuck overlay + wrong target element + no search).
- **CAD/MXN dropdowns ARE real** (corrects §2 "no CAD/MXN"): office **1605 renders 15** primary dropdowns (5 USD + 5 CAD + 5 MXN). Selector keys confirmed: `location-settings-select-primary-{labor,equipment,internal-equipment,production-labor,production-equipment}-pricing-{usd,cad,mxn}`. They render per-currency only where the office holds that currency's pricebook data → **office 1604 is USD-only (5 dropdowns); CAD/MXN are honest-absent on 1604 specifically, NOT app-wide.**
- **1604 is atypical:** its 5 USD primary dropdowns are all `--Select--` (unset). Office 1605's USD dropdowns are populated. → multi-currency CAD/MXN coverage (subplan mandate) should target a multi-currency office (e.g. 1605), not 1604.
