Rutvik directive (2026-07-11, after D12 promotion): "once u finish task, if i am not blocking u, DO NOT STOP for me to tell u to specifically do this.. its a part of the task execution! do not make me remind u each damn time!" — I had reported the prev task "functionally done, missing closure paperwork" and idled instead of doing the paperwork.

**Why:** Closure (post-exec audit + Receipt + reflect + log-of-record + /final-q) is not optional trailing work — it IS the task's tail. Stopping between functional-done and closure-done forces Rutvik to babysit, which is the exact opposite of the takeover system's purpose. This is the mid-session behavioral extension of LR-060 (no silent checkpoint): LR-060's Stop-hook only catches session END; this rule governs the moment functional work completes MID-session.

**How to apply:** The instant a task's functional work is verified, continue straight into its closure ceremonies in the same turn/flow — new user messages mid-task pause, they don't cancel; answer them and resume the chain. Only an explicit Rutvik block ("stop", "hold", "wait") halts closure. Related: [[feedback-no-rush-at-session-end]], [[feedback-plan-deviations-log]].
